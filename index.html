NoForget privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026. Applies to the NoForget Android app (ng.epoh.rouse).

NoForget reminds you to keep in touch with the people you choose and to do errands when you reach the right place. It has no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics of its own; the one exception is the optional Quick add below. Almost everything it knows stays on your phone.

What leaves your phone

Place lookups, when you use place features. The two optional features below, Google backup and Quick add, also send data while you have them on.

These services answer the request; NoForget does not ask them to store anything and keeps no copy of these requests anywhere but your phone. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

What stays on your phone

  • People and contacts: the people you add, read from your contacts when you pick them.
  • Call history: with your permission, NoForget reads recent calls on your phone and keeps a local record of them (number, time, length and direction) to notice when you have talked to someone you track. Only calls with people you track affect reminders. Call history is never sent anywhere, except into your own Google account if you turn on backup (below).
  • WhatsApp calls (optional, off by default): if you turn on "Calls from other apps", NoForget reads only WhatsApp's call notifications, on your phone, to count those calls.
  • Errands, places, settings and reminder history.
  • Your precise location: used on your phone to notice when you arrive at a place. Your exact location is only sent when you name your current spot: "Use current location", or confirming a map pin at the position the map opened on.

Backup to your Google account (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Back up to Google account" in Settings, Android's built-in backup stores your people, their call history, missed calls, errands and settings in your own Google account, encrypted with your screen lock, so they come back when you reinstall or set up a new phone. NoForget has no server and never sees the backup. Reminders, notifications and permissions are not part of it and are set up again on the new phone. If the phone has no screen lock, so the backup cannot be encrypted, NoForget does not back up to your account at all. Calls from numbers you do not track are kept only while needed to avoid counting a call twice, normally about a week, and they are part of the backup while they are kept. While the switch is off, NoForget hands nothing to Android's backup. Turning it off stops new backups; a copy already made stays in your Google account; you can delete it in Google Drive under Backups.

Quick add (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Quick add with on-device AI" in Settings, you can type a reminder in one sentence and your phone's own AI (Gemini Nano, through Google's ML Kit) fills in the reminder for you to check before saving. What you type is read on your phone and is not sent anywhere; a place it names is then searched like any place search (see above). While Quick add is on, Google's ML Kit sends Google diagnostics and usage data: device and app information, a per-installation identifier and error codes, as described in Google's ML Kit data disclosure. Google says it does not pass this data to third parties. If you never turn it on, ML Kit does not run and sends nothing. Turning it off stops ML Kit; diagnostics it had already queued may still be sent.

Permissions

NoForget asks for each permission when a feature needs it: location (including in the background, so arrival reminders work while the app is closed), call log, contacts, notifications, and optionally notification access for WhatsApp calls. Background location, call log and notification access each come with an explanation first. NoForget keeps working without a permission where it can, and you can revoke any of them in Android settings.

Sharing, selling and children

NoForget does not sell or share your data, show ads, or use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own; with Quick add on, Google's ML Kit sends Google the diagnostics described above. It is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Everything NoForget stores is on your phone. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes all of it from the phone. A Google backup, if you turned backup on, stays in your Google account until you delete it in Google Drive under Backups. NoForget keeps nothing on any server, so there is nothing else to request.
