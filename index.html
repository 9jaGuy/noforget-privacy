Rouse privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026. Applies to the Rouse Android app (ng.epoh.rouse).

Rouse reminds you to keep in touch with the people you choose and to do errands when you reach the right place. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Almost everything it knows stays on your phone.

What leaves your phone

Only place lookups, and only when you use place features:

Sent toWhatWhen
OpenStreetMap Photon (photon.komoot.io)The place name you type, and your location rounded to about 1 km so nearby results come firstWhen you search for a place
OpenStreetMap PhotonThe exact point you are namingWhen you drop a pin, or name your current spot ("Use current location", or a pin left where the map opened on you)
OpenStreetMap Overpass (overpass-api.de)A location rounded to about 110 m, the kinds of place or the store name you chose, and a search radiusWhen you have an errand for "any place of a type"
OpenStreetMap map tiles (tile.openstreetmap.org)The map area on screenWhile the pin-drop map is open
Your phone's own map service (on most phones, Google)The same search or point as aboveOnly when OpenStreetMap can't be reached

These services answer the request; Rouse does not ask them to store anything and keeps no copy of these requests anywhere but your phone. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

What stays on your phone

  • People and contacts: the people you add, read from your contacts when you pick them.
  • Call history: with your permission, Rouse reads recent calls on your phone and keeps a local record of them (number, time, length and direction) to notice when you have talked to someone you track. Only calls with people you track affect reminders. Call history is never sent anywhere.
  • WhatsApp calls (optional, off by default): if you turn on "Calls from other apps", Rouse reads only WhatsApp's call notifications, on your phone, to count those calls.
  • Errands, places, settings and reminder history.
  • Your precise location: used on your phone to notice when you arrive at a place. Your exact location is only sent when you name your current spot: "Use current location", or confirming a map pin at the position the map opened on.

Permissions

Rouse asks for each permission when a feature needs it: location (including in the background, so arrival reminders work while the app is closed), call log, contacts, notifications, and optionally notification access for WhatsApp calls. Background location, call log and notification access each come with an explanation first. Rouse keeps working without a permission where it can, and you can revoke any of them in Android settings.

Sharing, selling and children

Rouse does not sell or share your data, show ads or use analytics or crash-reporting services. It is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Everything Rouse stores is on your phone. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes all of it. Rouse keeps nothing on any server, so there is nothing else to request.
